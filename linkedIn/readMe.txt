--------------------------
For each day:
1. Find the shloka below in its original book/scripture

3. Come to Claude with:
   - The deity image (JPG/PNG)
   - The typed/written shloka text (copy-paste Devanagari)
   - Say: "Generate Day X PDF — here is my image and shloka"

DAY 1 — LORD GANESHA
Date to post: 23 June 2026
Theme: New Beginnings
Scripture: Ganesha Atharvasirsha (Atharvasirsha Upanishad)
Part of: Atharvaveda
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FIND IN BOOK:
  Book: Ganesha Atharvasirsha (also called Ganapati Atharvasirsha)
  It is a short Upanishad — only about 16-18 verses total.
  Look for verse beginning with "त्वमेव केवलं"
  Available in: "108 Upanishads" compiled by Pt. Shriram Sharma Acharya
                OR standalone "Ganapati Atharvasirsha" booklets

SHLOKA TO COPY:
  त्वमेव केवलं कर्तासि ।
  त्वमेव केवलं धर्तासि ।
  त्वमेव केवलं हर्तासि ।
  त्वमेव सर्वं खल्विदं ब्रह्मासि ।
  त्वं साक्षादात्मासि नित्यम् ॥

ENGLISH MEANING:
  You alone are the Creator. You alone are the Sustainer.
  You alone are the Destroyer. You are everything —
  You are Brahman itself. You are the eternal Self, always.

DAY 2 — LORD KRISHNA
Date to post: 24 June 2026
Theme: Karma & Action
Scripture: Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 2, Verse 47
Part of: Mahabharata (Bhishma Parva)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FIND IN BOOK:
  Book: Bhagavad Gita — any edition (Gita Press Gorakhpur recommended)
  Chapter 2 (Sankhya Yoga), Verse 47
  One of the most printed verses — easy to find in any Gita

SHLOKA TO COPY:
  कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
  मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥

ENGLISH MEANING:
  You have the right to perform your prescribed duties,
  but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions.
  Never consider yourself the cause of results,
  and never be attached to not doing your duty.

SHLOKA TO COPY:
  शान्ताकारं भुजगशयनं पद्मनाभं सुरेशम् ।
  विश्वाधारं गगनसदृशं मेघवर्णं शुभाङ्गम् ।

ENGLISH MEANING:
  I salute Vishnu — whose form is peace, who rests on the
  serpent, from whose navel blooms the lotus, lord of gods,
  support of the universe, sky-like, cloud-coloured,
  beloved of Lakshmi, lotus-eyed, reachable by yogis in
  meditation — who destroys the fear of worldly existence.
